The detail

The parts that actually matter

Why length changes the maths

A ~290-page full-length novel is roughly 1.31 USD of KU page reads per complete read, against about 0.29 USD for a ~65-page short. Same reader, same click — four and a half times the payout. That is before the higher list price.

  • Short ~65 pages ≈ $0.29 per full read
  • Standard ~160 pages ≈ $0.72 per full read
  • Full-length ~290 pages ≈ $1.31 per full read
  • Ebook at $4.99 / 70% ≈ $3.49 per sale

Box sets are a second product from the same work

Once you have 2+ books in a series, Box Set Builder produces a separate listing at a higher price point — with its own cover, TOC and description. No new writing. A 5-book box set at $9.99 at 70% is roughly $6.99 per sale.

  • Combined TOC generated for you
  • Per-book divider pages
  • Flat or 3D box set cover
  • AI-written box set description

A real catalogue, not a test

Unlimited active series is what turns three experiments into a catalogue: different genres, different pen names, all cross-promoting. Ten full-length books is roughly 2,900 KENP pages of surface area against roughly 325 for a five-book short series.
