This matters more than any feature list, so it is stated plainly: AI-narrated audio is not accepted by Audible through ACX today. BookSaga audio is not for ACX upload. It is for the stores and channels that do accept it.

  • Reader magnet — a free audio prequel to grow your list
  • Always re-check each store's current AI-narration policy before publishing

What 'mastered to ACX specs' means

It means the technical envelope is already inside the range that professional audio platforms expect — so the files are not rejected on technical grounds, and they sound consistent from chapter one to chapter thirty.

  • RMS -23 to -18 dB
  • True peak at or below -3 dB
  • Noise floor at or below -60 dB
  • Room tone between chapters
  • Opening and closing credit files

Questions people actually ask

Can I upload this to Audible / ACX?

No. ACX requires human narration, and AI-narrated audio is not accepted there today. This is stated plainly so nobody buys expecting otherwise. Use it for Google Play Books, Kobo, your own store and reader magnets instead.

Which stores do accept AI-narrated audio?

Google Play Books accepts it with disclosure, Kobo accepts it when the narrator is listed as a synthetic voice, and your own store accepts whatever you sell. Store policies change — verify before each launch.

Do audio credits come out of my main credits?

No. Audio is a separate pool. 15 audio credits per book, and your main writing credits are untouched.

What files do I get?

One MP3 per chapter at 192 kbps CBR, 44.1 kHz mono, plus opening and closing credit files, a 2400×2400 square cover, and a ZIP of everything.

Can I preview a voice first?

Yes. Every voice has a preview sample, and you can re-preview at different speeds before committing credits.

What if the voice is not right?

Re-generate with a different voice. Credits are only spent on actual generation, and exports are always free.
